A great bar with
a broken front door.

Welch Ave. Station has been a Campustown staple for more than 32 years, but its website hasn't kept up. This semester we audited the site, then built a display ad campaign and a new landing page to fix what we found.

Executive summary

What we did this semester.

The business. Welch Ave. Station is a bar at 207 Welch Ave. in Ames, owned longer than any other bar in Campustown. Its strengths are real: no cover charge all night, the lowest regular prices in Campustown and a loyal college crowd.

The audit. Our five-person team reviewed the Home, Location, Specials and Food pages for user interface, user experience, on-site SEO and performance. We found a strong bar with a weak digital front door.

The campaign. We turned the audit into a "No cover. No catch." display campaign aimed at 21+ Iowa State students, sports fans and local service workers who are looking for a good time without the extra cost.

The landing page. Every ad links to a new, mobile-friendly landing page that matches the ad word for word and applies our audit's recommendations, from clearer calls to action to easier mobile navigation.

Part 1 · Website audit

What we found on welchave.com.

We evaluated four pages (Home, Location, Specials and Food) from four angles.

User interface

Jack Norby

The only consistent element is the bright blue background. Buttons, images, trust symbols and even the phone number are missing from most pages, and the header with the bar's name and address disappears under the fold.

User experience

Jose Velazquez & Carlos Rodríguez

All five of us finished three visitor tasks with the left sidebar, so the basic navigation works. But the Pizza Pit menu was the slowest to reach and hard to read as an embedded PDF, and key information was scattered.

On-site SEO

Kaden Klein

Our five target keyword phrases already appear naturally, with no keyword stuffing, and every page is indexed. But there are no meta descriptions or canonical tags, the Home and Food pages have weak titles, and the site isn't mobile-friendly.

Performance

John Pezley

Speed is a strength: every page scored high on Lighthouse for load time and SEO on both mobile and desktop. The gaps are no HTTPS, no mobile layout (pages are shrunk desktop versions), and a few missing accessibility details.

Our recommendations

Rebuild it modern, mobile and trustworthy.

We recommended a full redesign that drives foot traffic from the website to the bar. Our landing page is a working first step.

Built into our landing page

  • Mobile-friendly, responsive layout
  • A navigation bar on every page
  • Served over HTTPS through GitHub Pages
  • Clear calls to action: specials, directions, call
  • Our five keyword phrases in real page text, plus meta descriptions
  • The Pizza Pit menu as readable text, not a PDF
  • Phone number and address on every screen
  • Alt text on every image and a valid HTML5 doctype
Part 2 · Display ad campaign

No cover. No catch.

Goal: local brand awareness. Audience: 21+ Iowa State students, sports fans and Ames service-industry workers. Hook: "No cover. No catch." Budget: $600 over four weeks.

The ads run in four sizes, and every one links to the landing page with its own tracking tag.
