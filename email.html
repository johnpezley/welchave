Step 2 · List-building strategy

Three groups, three ways in.

We'll grow the list where our customers already are: at the bar, on Facebook, and on the sites where our display ads run. Every sign-up gets the same immediate reward, a free slice, so the offer is easy to promote everywhere.

Group 1 · In person

Customers in the bar

Where & how we collect it

Touchpoint: QR codes on table tents, coasters and the check presenter. The code opens the sign-up form at the top of this page.

How we reach them: In person. Bartenders mention the free slice when someone opens a tab or shows their ID at the door.

Immediate value

A free slice of Pizza Pit pizza, sent as soon as they confirm their email and good at the bar that same night.

Long-term value

A free birthday drink, the week's specials every Wednesday, Pizza Pit deals and first word on events.

Group 2 · Social

Facebook followers

Where & how we collect it

Touchpoint: A pinned giveaway post and the Sign Up button on Welch's Facebook page: "Join our email list for a chance to win a reserved game-day table for you and five friends."

How we reach them: Organic posts, a small boosted post shown to people 21+ within 5 miles of Campustown, and user-generated content: tag Welch in a photo for a bonus entry.

Immediate value

Instant entry into that month's game-day table giveaway, plus the free-slice voucher.

Long-term value

A subscriber-only giveaway every month, game-day deals and event reminders before they're posted on Facebook.

Group 3 · Paid

People who click our Google ads

Where & how we collect it

Touchpoint: Retargeting. People who clicked our "No cover. No catch." display ads and visited the landing page later see a second ad, "Join the list, get a free slice," that links straight to this sign-up form.

How we reach them: Google Display Network remarketing, with the same 21+ Ames targeting as our display campaign.

Immediate value

The free-slice voucher, plus this week's specials in their inbox right away.

Long-term value

The week's specials every Wednesday so they never have to look them up, plus a free birthday drink.
